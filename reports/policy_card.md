# Policy card

Seven policies span control, static, CTR-based, relevance-constrained, CATE, and AIX-constrained choices. User-level 60/20/20 discovery, selection, and locked-test roles separate fitting, threshold/comparator choice, and final OPE. Policy safety is estimated directly for user utility, abandonment, organic engagement, low-relevance exposure, and density.

The frozen P6 AIX candidate was safe, but its locked DR advertiser value was 0.017976 versus 0.018710 for P1, the frozen strongest safe baseline—a relative difference of -3.93%. P6 was not promoted. Oracle evidence was opened only after selection and could not change the policy.
