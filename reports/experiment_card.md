# Experiment card

The controlled semi-synthetic experiment randomized persistent simulated users into 12 format × position × density cells. Its frozen size was 16,977,840 sessions, powered at 1,414,820 per arm from an independent qualification pilot. Five hundred A/A replications yielded 5.6% Type-I error and 94.4% 95%-interval coverage.

User-level SRM passed at p=0.9281; assignment, contamination, missingness, balance, and traffic checks passed. The frozen static finalist increased simulated advertiser value by 0.007125 (40.90%; 95% CI 0.006513–0.007738), passed every frozen finalist guardrail, and remained positive across six pseudo-weeks.
