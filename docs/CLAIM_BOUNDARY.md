# Claim boundary

## Supported descriptions

- public KDD Cup 2012 Track 2 sponsored-search modeling
- offline public-data model evaluation
- controlled semi-synthetic randomized experiment
- simulated advertiser value and user/page outcomes
- locked offline policy evaluation
- portfolio research benchmark

## Unsupported descriptions

- Google Ads experiment or Google Search traffic
- Google proprietary or internal data
- production advertiser or revenue lift
- deployed production policy
- reproduction of Google internal methodology

The 149.6M-row scale describes labeled public KDD aggregate observations. The 17.0M-session scale describes controlled semi-synthetic experiment sessions. They are not the same dataset and are never presented as production evidence.
